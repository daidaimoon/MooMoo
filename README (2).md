# 🐱 MooMoo — Your Browser Cat Companion

A tuxedo cat that lives in the corner of your browser.

She won't track your productivity, won't pop up reminders to drink water, and won't help you manage your to-do list. She just quietly stays there — taking a stroll now and then, curling up for a nap, or bringing you a little letter in her mouth.

During all those long hours you spend staring at a screen, she's a small, soft, living presence.

![MooMoo Walking](screenshots/walk.png)

MooMoo is a Chrome extension, hand-drawn in Japanese watercolor ink style. She has her own little life: stretching in the morning, eating at noon, chasing butterflies in the afternoon, and curling up to sleep late at night. You don't need to do anything — she's just there, keeping you company.

---

## What Does She Do?

### 🎨 22 Hand-Drawn Animations

Walking, sleeping, stretching, yawning, eating, drinking, grooming, chasing butterflies, playing with a feather wand, lounging, sprawling out, rolling around, getting startled… Each animation was generated as an AI video and then extracted frame by frame — so she's truly *moving*, not just flipping between static images.

![MooMoo Sleeping](screenshots/sleep.png)
![MooMoo Sitting](screenshots/sit.png)

### ⏰ She Knows What Time It Is

She stretches and yawns in the morning, eats and drinks at noon, gets playful in the afternoon chasing butterflies and batting at things, and sleeps quietly late at night. It's not a rigid schedule — more like a tendency. She has her own randomness, and you never know what she'll do next.

Just like a real cat, she has her own rhythm. And that uncontrollable sense of a little life doing its own thing is exactly what makes her feel warm.

### 💛 Your Relationship Grows Over Time

When you first install MooMoo, she doesn't know you yet. Click on her and she'll get startled and flinch. But as long as you keep opening your browser every day, she'll slowly let her guard down, start talking to you, and eventually even roll over to show you her belly.

There's nothing you need to do — just let time pass, and trust grows on its own. Just like building a relationship with a real cat: you can't rush it, but when you look back, you'll realize everything has been quietly changing.

| Stage | Approximate Time | How She Acts |
|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| 🐱 Stranger | First 2 weeks | Cautious, gets startled when clicked |
| 👀 Curious | Weeks 3-4 | Occasionally pops up "?" or "...", watching you |
| 😺 Familiar | Months 2-3 | Starts saying "Meow~", no longer afraid |
| 💛 Close | Month 3 | Greets you on her own |
| 🌟 Happy Buddies | After month 3 | Fully trusts you, shows her belly |

### 📬 A Little Challenge Every Week

Every Monday, MooMoo brings you a letter in her mouth. Inside is a random little challenge —

> 🍹 "Try a drink you've never had before and take a photo to capture the moment"
>
> 💌 "Write a short letter to yourself 10 years from now"
>
> ☁️ "Learn to identify three different types of clouds"

These challenges aren't KPIs. No scores, no leaderboards. They're just gentle little nudges to lift your head from the screen and take a closer look at everyday life. You can accept them, write a quick reflection when you're done, or skip them — no penalty at all.

If you set up a DeepSeek API key, challenges will be dynamically generated by AI, different every time. No key? No problem — MooMoo comes with 55 carefully crafted built-in challenges.

### 📊 Monthly Browsing Report

MooMoo quietly keeps track of which websites you visit each day (domain-level only — no specific pages, no data uploaded anywhere). On the 1st of each month, she brings you a monthly report to help you see where your time went.

Not to make you anxious — just to help you see yourself.

### 🗄️ Collection Cabinet

As your relationship deepens, MooMoo will occasionally bring you little gifts — a fallen leaf, a feather, a butterfly, a star… 20 collectibles in total, across four rarity tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, and Legendary. Right-click MooMoo to open the collection cabinet and see how many you've gathered.

Each one is something she picked up on her little strolls. A small cat's way of saying she cares.

![Collection Cabinet](screenshots/collection.png)

### 🔧 DeepSeek API (Optional)

Right-click MooMoo → Settings to enter your DeepSeek API Key. Once configured, weekly challenges will be dynamically generated by AI. No key? Everything else works perfectly fine.

The API Key is stored only in the browser session — it's automatically cleared when you close the browser and is never uploaded anywhere.

![Settings Panel](screenshots/settings.png)

## 📦 How to Install

MooMoo isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet. Manual installation is easy:

1. Click the green **Code** button at the top right of this page → **Download ZIP**
2. Unzip to a folder you can find
3. Open Chrome, type `chrome://extensions/` in the address bar
4. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right toggle)
5. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder

Open any webpage and you'll see her in the bottom right corner 🐱

## 🎨 Why Did I Make This?

We spend so much of our time staring at screens. Working, studying, scrolling through feeds — during all of that, the screen is cold, mechanical, functional.

I wanted to put something alive into that coldness.

MooMoo isn't a productivity tool, isn't an AI assistant, isn't anything you need to "use." She's just a cat, living life at her own pace. You do your thing, she does hers. You glance down and see her sleeping, and somehow that's comforting.

She can't help you finish your work. But maybe she can make those hours in front of the screen feel just a little bit warmer.

That's enough.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Reference images: Microsoft Copilot (DALL-E 3)
